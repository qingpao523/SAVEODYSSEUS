Core loop

Simple to read. Hard to leave.

A small board, a clear threat, and a hero who cannot be everywhere. Every decision is spatial, temporal, or both.

Systems

Six systems, one pressure clock.

01

Merge crew

Hoplite, Archer, Sailor, Shield Wall, Ballista. Two identical units become one stronger unit; coverage shrinks, power spikes.

02

Move Odysseus

Three lanes. Four-second move cooldown. Spear, Aegis, and Athena's Light. The hero is a decision, not a stat block.

03

Hold the hull

Three hull sections. Every leak breaks one. A Sailor can repair once per wave. Zero hull ends the run.

04

Beat the storm

180-second timer. Red at 2:30. At 3:00 the storm deals hull damage every six seconds. A comeback window, not an instant loss.

05

Choose a god

Athena, Poseidon, Hermes, Hephaestus, Apollo, Circe. Boons change behavior, not just percentages.

06

Face the Cyclops

Rock throws, slam, eye window. Save the ultimate for the open eye. Do not tell him your name.

Web-first

Built for the first ten seconds.

CrazyGames is the launch platform. The game must load fast, read at a glance, and feel complete on a 4GB Chromebook and a mid-tier phone.

  • Stack: Phaser 3 + TypeScript + Vite; DOM/CSS HUD overlay.
  • Budget: 15-18MB first build; first playable under 10 seconds.
  • Layout: landscape primary; portrait reflow; 44px touch targets.
  • SDK: loading, gameplayStart/Stop, save, mute, locale, end-of-run ads.
  • No: energy, login wall, pay-to-win, ads during battle, custom fullscreen.
Audience

Global by default.

The game is English-first, but the visual promise is culture-light: a ship, a monster, a hero, a storm. The Greek surface is specific; the emotion is universal.

  • Primary: tower defense, merge, and short-session players.
  • Secondary: Greek myth fans and score chasers.
  • Market: US, Bangladesh, India, Australia, Brazil; desktop and mobile.
  • Session: 10-15 minutes, 3-4 runs.

Emotional promise

Not kill monsters.

The sea takes everything; you bring them home. Every run is one leg of the voyage. Ithaca is always in the distance. Failure is the Fates reweaving the route, not a canon death.

  • Ship: the emotional anchor, not a health bar.
  • Crew: flawed, funny, worth saving.
  • Odysseus: clever, tired, sometimes too proud.
  • Gods: forces with opinions, not mascots.
