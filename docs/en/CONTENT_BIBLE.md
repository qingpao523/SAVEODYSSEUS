# SAVE ODYSSEUS - Content Bible

Version: 0.1
Scope: MVP and full v1 content baseline

## 1. Canon boundary

Use Homer's *Odyssey* and Greek myth as the public-domain source. The following are safe: Odysseus, Penelope, Telemachus, Athena, Poseidon, Zeus, Hermes, Circe, Calypso, the Cyclops Polyphemus, the Sirens, Scylla, Charybdis, the Laestrygonians, the Lotus-Eaters, Ithaca, Troy, the Aegean, and the ship.

Do not copy modern translations, films, novels, games, character designs, music, UI, or unique plot additions. Do not use *Assassin's Creed Odyssey*, *Super Mario Odyssey*, or their branding. Write original dialogue and descriptions.

## 2. Tone

Heroic, wry, and humane. Odysseus is clever, tired, and sometimes too proud. Athena is dry and precise. Poseidon is a force, not a cartoon villain. The crew complains, jokes, and makes mistakes. The sea is beautiful and dangerous.

No gore, no sexual content, no gambling aesthetics. PEGI12. Violence is stylized: monsters burst into black-figure patterns or sea foam, not blood.

## 3. Story frame

Athena speaks to the player between runs: "The Fates have woven this route again. Bring him home." Each run is one attempt at a leg of the voyage. Failure is not canon death; it is the loom resetting. This justifies replay without undoing the story.

The full voyage has nine islands. The MVP has one: the Cyclops cave. The player does not need to know the epic to understand the goal: survive the sea, protect the ship, reach Ithaca.

## 4. Main characters

Odysseus: the player avatar. A captain, not a superhero. His strength is cunning, but the game abstracts it into lane movement and timing. He does not speak much during battle; short barks only.

Athena: narrator and patron. She explains mechanics without long tutorials. She respects the player. She never says "click here" in a condescending way.

Poseidon: pressure, storm, and risk. He is not a boss in the MVP, but his storm timer is always present.

Penelope and Telemachus: off-screen emotional stakes. They appear in the map screen and the final Ithaca node. No damsel framing; Penelope is clever and holds the household together.

## 9. Boss: Polyphemus

Fantasy: a huge silhouette in the cave mouth; rocks, dust, and a single glowing eye. He is dangerous but not evil; the player is trespassing.

Phase 1 (100-70%): walks down center, throws a rock at a telegraphed lane every 8s. Rock deals 1 hull damage unless blocked by Aegis or a Shield Wall.

Phase 2 (70-40%): summons two waves of raiders from the side lanes; slam attack hits adjacent tiles.

Phase 3 (40-0%): enraged; rock every 5s; after each slam, the eye opens for 3s and takes double damage. The player should save Athena's Light for this window.

MVP boss HP: 6000. Boss armor: 20% against non-ultimate damage. The eye window removes armor.

## 10. Future bosses

Sirens: charm crew; the player taps charmed units to break the song. Scylla: three heads attack three lanes; the player must choose which lane to shield. Laestrygonians: horde boss; ballista and AoE shine. Poseidon storm: environmental boss; the ship must survive a timed sequence.

## 11. UI copy and voice

Use short, confident English. Avoid faux-archaic "thou" and "thee". Avoid generic fantasy filler.

Examples:

- Home: "The sea is waiting."
- Map: "One more island. One more chance."
- Summon: "Call the crew."
- Merge: "Stronger together."
- Storm warning: "The storm is turning."
- Boss intro: "Do not tell him your name."
- Victory: "Ithaca is closer."
- Defeat: "The Fates reweave the route."
- Result tip: "You lost the left lane. Try a Hoplite there next time."

Athena barks should be dry, not maternal. Odysseus barks should be tired, not quippy. The crew can be funny; the gods should not be memes.

## 12. Content cadence

MVP: one island, one boss, five units, six boons, six enemy types, one hero, one async duel.

Full v1: nine islands, five bosses, twelve crew, six gods, thirty-six boons, twelve enemy types, three ship skins, daily sea, weekly leaderboard.

Post-launch: one new island per season, one new boss, two crew, one god, cosmetic set. No stat-selling battle pass.
