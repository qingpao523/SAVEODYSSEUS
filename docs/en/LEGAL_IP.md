# SAVE ODYSSEUS - Legal and IP Notes

Version: 0.1
This is a production risk note, not legal advice.

## 1. What is public domain

Homer's *Odyssey*, Greek myth, the names Odysseus, Penelope, Telemachus, Athena, Poseidon, the Cyclops, the Sirens, Scylla, Charybdis, Ithaca, and Troy are public-domain source material. A game may adapt them without a license.

## 2. What is not public domain

- Modern translations of the *Odyssey*. Use a public-domain translation or write original text.
- Modern novels, films, television, comics, and games based on the *Odyssey*, including their dialogue, character designs, music, and unique plot additions.
- *Assassin's Creed Odyssey* and *Super Mario Odyssey*: names, logos, characters, art, music, UI, and marketing are protected.
- Any actor likeness, film still, or soundtrack.

## 3. Title risk

The word "Odyssey" is used by many games and brands. **SAVE ODYSSEUS** is the working title because it is clear English and more distinctive than "The Odyssey". Before launch:

1. Search the title in the USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, and target-market databases for game software classes.
2. Search CrazyGames, Steam, Google Play, and the App Store for confusingly similar titles.
3. Keep backup names: NOSTOS: Save Odysseus, Aegean Oath, The Long Way Home.
4. Do not use "Odyssey" with a subtitle or logo that resembles *Assassin's Creed Odyssey* or *Super Mario Odyssey*.

## 4. Originality rules

- All art, animation, UI, music, SFX, code, and copy are created for this project or licensed.
- Do not trace or copy another game's character, ship, monster, or UI.
- Do not use AI-generated art that resembles a known copyrighted character or living artist's style.
- Keep a source file and license record for every external asset.
- Do not use "inspired by [game]" in the store page or keywords if it implies endorsement.

## 5. CrazyGames compliance

- No external ads, external links, app-store links, or custom fullscreen.
- No ads during gameplay; midgame ads only at a natural break.
- No forced rewarded revive.
- English localization required.
- PEGI12 content; no gore, sexual content, or gambling.
- No misleading title, icon, or screenshots.
- Respect privacy; no personal data collection without consent.

## 6. Credits

Include a credits screen with art, audio, code, and localization credits. If using open-source libraries, include their licenses in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## 7. China mobile release note

Mobile-CN is a separate compliance track. Before commercial release in China, confirm game approval number, real-name verification, anti-addiction, privacy policy, data handling, payment compliance, ad review, and Android channel requirements. Do not use the global mobile build as a compliance shortcut.
